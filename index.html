🛡️ Fair rewards, safer trading

Points are awarded only after verified completed transactions. Refunds and chargebacks reverse points. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, artificial trades and review manipulation are prohibited. Referral rewards have limits; purchase discounts cannot be exchanged for cash. Country-specific eligibility, expiry terms, privacy and consumer rules will be published before launch.

How SWITCH works

Trading should be simple, flexible and fair.

1

Find something you love

Browse listings and visit other members' collections. Sellers accept offers by default unless they turn them off.

2

Make your offer

Offer one item, several items, cash, or a combination. The seller can accept, decline or counteroffer.

3

Agree and exchange

Confirm the exact deal, arrange delivery and track the exchange. Trade protection is planned for a later release.
